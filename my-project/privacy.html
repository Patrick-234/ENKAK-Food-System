Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

We collect your name, email address, delivery address and order history solely to process and deliver your orders and to let you track them.

Your payment details are processed securely and are not stored on our servers.

We do not sell or share your personal information with third parties, except delivery riders, who receive only the details needed to complete a delivery — your name, phone number and delivery address, never your payment information or order history beyond the current delivery.

We may use your email to send order updates and, where you've opted in, occasional news about ENkak. You can opt out of non-essential communications at any time from your Account settings.

We retain your order history so you can view past orders and reorder easily. You may request a copy of your data or ask us to delete your account at any time by contacting support.

This policy may be updated from time to time; significant changes will be communicated to you directly.

Questions about your data? Contact us.
