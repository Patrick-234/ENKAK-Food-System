Quick answers

How do I track my order? +

Open your Account page and tap the order under "Active Order". You'll see it move from Confirmed to Preparing to Out for Delivery to Delivered.

Can I cancel an order? +

Orders can be cancelled before the kitchen starts preparing them. Once preparation begins, cancellation may not be possible. Send us a complaint with your order number and we'll help.

My payment went through but my order didn't show up +

Send us a complaint with the order number or the time of payment. We'll confirm the payment and fix it.

How do I change my account details? +

Go to your Account page. If something can't be changed there, contact us and we'll update it for you.
