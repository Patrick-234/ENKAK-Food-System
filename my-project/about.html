OUR STORY

ENkak started with a simple idea: ordering food shouldn't feel like a transaction – it should feel like someone's looking out for you. Whether it's a steaming plate of jollof rice after a long day or a cold Chapman for the weekend, we wanted a platform that treats every order like it matters.

We work with trusted local kitchens to bring you meals, snacks and drinks made fresh, and we pair every order with a dedicated delivery rider so you always know exactly where your food is and when it's arriving.

Our promise is simple: good food, honest pricing, and a delivery experience you can actually rely on – one order at a time.

What we stand for

OUR VISION

To become a trusted and recognizable Nigerian food and pastry brand, known for quality, accessibility, innovation and memorable customer experiences.

OUR MISSION

To make everyday moments warmer through delicious, affordable and consistently prepared pastries, supported by convenient services and a commitment to customer satisfaction.

THE JOURNEY AHEAD

We are starting one customer at a time, one/ order at a time and one experience at a time.

We know that building something meaningful takes patience, discipline and consistency. We are committed to earning our customers' trust rather than simply asking for it.
